Agile Bear and Aloe

About the Team

Agile Bear is a quick-thinking, quick-moving hero who combines the raw strength of a bear with unmatched speed and reflexes. Despite his size, nothing about Agile Bear is slow or clumsy — he can dodge, climb, and strike faster than opponents expect. His sidekick, Aloe, provides support and healing in the field, using natural remedies to patch up the team after tough battles and keep them going when the fight gets long.

Foes

  • Static Serpent
    Major power: Can freeze time for up to 10 seconds.
    Minor power: Leaves a faint electric buzzing sound wherever it slithers.
  • The Hollow Man
    Major power: Can turn invisible at will.
    Minor power: Always smells faintly of smoke.

How to Make a Paper Airplane

  1. Fold the paper in half
    1. Place a sheet of letter-size paper horizontally.
    2. Fold it lengthwise so the long edges meet.
    3. Crease the fold firmly, then unfold the paper.
  2. Create the nose
    1. Fold the top-left corner down toward the center crease.
    2. Fold the top-right corner down toward the center crease.
    3. Make sure the two folded edges meet along the center line.
  3. Make the wings
    1. Fold the slanted upper edges toward the center crease again.
    2. Fold the entire airplane in half along the original center crease.
    3. Fold each side downward to form the wings.
      1. Leave a narrow strip along the bottom for the airplane's body.
      2. Make both wings as symmetrical as possible.
  4. Finish the airplane
    1. Run your fingers along every crease to sharpen them.
    2. Hold the airplane by the body underneath the wings.
    3. Give it a gentle, level throw.
    4. Adjust the wing folds if the airplane dives or stalls.
